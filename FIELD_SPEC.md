## The sense

"Hyperemergent = newly emerging cross-level coherence before it stabilizes
into a named hyperrelation, graph, or hyperform."

[FORM] The tests check:

- `hyperemergent` resolves, by exact name, to one sense, and that sense names
  its source.
- `hyperemergence`, the field's name and the word Tyler uses, is a second

## The finite reading

[FRAME] The definition makes two conditions checkable on a declaration, and
leaves two unchecked:

- A `Candidate` has a non-empty name, 1 to 64 named levels and at most one
  `Stabilization`. A level is a non-empty name. A number, including a Boolean,
  is refused.
- A `Stabilization` is into one of the three targets the definition names, a
  hyperrelation, a graph or a hyperform, and it carries the target's name.
- `assess` returns REFUTED when the candidate spans one level ("cross-level"
  fails) or is declared stabilized ("before it stabilizes" fails). It reports
  every reason that applies.
- Otherwise `assess` returns UNKNOWN, with three reasons: no criterion
  establishes the coherence, none establishes the newness, and a stabilization
  nobody declared is not evidence that none occurred.
- There is no confirming verdict. A candidate is a site, not an instance.

A stabilized candidate is read as the hyperrelation, graph or hyperform it
became. Whether it was a hyperemergent before is not recoverable from the
declaration. The candidate has no closure flag: "the realities loop and close
themselves" is recorded as Tyler's open statement, not as a field that could
stand in for a demonstration.

The two verdicts and their reasons are this repository's reading of the
definition; see [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md). They are bookkeeping that any
claim of hyperemergence would have to pass. They are not hyperemergence.

## Questions for Tyler

- **Q1.** Does Hyperstratum's definition move here, or stay in Hyperstratum
  and get cited here?
- **Q2.** Can one field be a hyperemergence from another? You asked whether
  Hyperchemistry can be defined as a hyperemergence from Hyperphysics, "Not
  yet at least?". The cited definition is about coherence before it
  stabilizes into a named hyperrelation, graph or hyperform. If a named field
  counts as one of those, it is past that moment under the definition, and
  the question becomes whether it emerged as one. The frame does not decide
  it.
- **Q3.** What confirms a hyperemergent? The definition gives no criterion
  for coherence or newness, so the frame can refute a candidate and never
  confirm one. Hypersubjectivity proposes a novelty target: against an
  uncoupled baseline, "a linked whole exhibits a refutable cross-level
  coherence that no isolated component or renamed edge already has". Is that
  the criterion, or is there another?
- **Q4.** On 2026-09-27 you asked what hyperemergence does with
  hyperstructure, hyperdynamics, hypergrammar, hypermath and hyperlogic, and
  asked for Hyperchemistry to be checked against Hypermath and Hyperlogic.
  What does each field contribute?
- **Q5.** What is a level? The frame takes levels as declared names. The
  candidates in the sources are the hyperstack's layers, the five realities
  you named on 2026-09-27, and the fields themselves.
- **Q6.** You said "from hyperethics we then can show that consciousness
  emerges novelties from hyperobjects". Is that novelty a hyperemergent in
  Hyperstratum's sense, and which field shows it: Hyperethics, as the
  sentence says, Hypersubjectivity, or this one?
